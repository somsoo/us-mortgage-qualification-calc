Frequently Asked Questions

How do mortgage lenders calculate maximum loan qualification?

Lenders evaluate your gross monthly income, monthly recurring debt obligations, down payment, and credit score using front-end and back-end debt-to-income ratios.

What is the 28/36 qualifying rule for conventional mortgages?

The 28/36 rule recommends that your monthly housing expense not exceed 28 percent of gross income, and total debt payments not exceed 36 percent.

How does down payment size affect mortgage loan qualification?

A larger down payment increases your total purchase purchasing power, lowers monthly interest payments, and eliminates private mortgage insurance (PMI) if reaching 20 percent.
