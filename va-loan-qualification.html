Key Fannie Mae, HUD/FHA & CFPB Underwriting Checkpoints

  • Fannie Mae Selling Guide (B3-6-02) & QM Rules: Conventional loans establish baseline qualifying limits of 28% front-end and 36% back-end DTI, extendable up to 45%–50% with automated underwriting compensating factors.
  • HUD Handbook 4000.1 (FHA Underwriting): FHA financing expands accessibility with higher 31% front-end and 43% back-end benchmarks with down payments as low as 3.5%.
  • Homeowners Protection Act (12 U.S.C. § 4901): Private mortgage insurance (PMI) is legally cancellable at 80% LTV and automatically terminates at 78% LTV, saving hundreds monthly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does VA loan mortgage qualification differ from Conventional loans?

VA loans require 0% down payment, charge no monthly private mortgage insurance (PMI), and use a flexible 41% total DTI benchmark alongside residual income requirements.

Who is eligible for VA home loan financing?

Active-duty service members, veterans, and qualifying surviving spouses who meet minimum service length requirements and obtain a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) from the VA.

What is the VA residual income requirement?

The VA requires borrowers to retain a minimum amount of discretionary cash each month after covering PITI and recurring debts to pay for food, utilities, and living expenses.
