Key Fannie Mae, HUD/FHA & CFPB Underwriting Checkpoints

  • Fannie Mae Selling Guide (B3-6-02) & QM Rules: Conventional loans establish baseline qualifying limits of 28% front-end and 36% back-end DTI, extendable up to 45%–50% with automated underwriting compensating factors.
  • HUD Handbook 4000.1 (FHA Underwriting): FHA financing expands accessibility with higher 31% front-end and 43% back-end benchmarks with down payments as low as 3.5%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much mortgage can I qualify for with a $60,000 salary?

At a $60,000 annual income ($5,000 gross monthly), conventional 28/36 DTI guidelines typically cap your total PITI mortgage payment at $1,400 per month, assuming moderate recurring debts.

What is the minimum down payment recommended on a $60k income?

Conventional loans allow as little as 3% down ($6,000 to $9,000 on typical starter homes), while FHA requires 3.5%, though saving 20% eliminates ongoing private mortgage insurance (PMI).

How do monthly car and credit card debts affect $60k qualification?

Every $100 in recurring monthly debt directly lowers your allowable mortgage payment under the 36% back-end limit, reducing your home purchasing power by roughly $15,000.
